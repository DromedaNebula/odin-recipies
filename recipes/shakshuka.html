Shakshuka

Description

Showcasing the simple yet spectacularly delectable cuisine from the African and Middle Eastern region, Shakshuka is a must have breakfast dish. This breakfast dish brings in lucious savory sauce derived from tomatoes, chili, peppers, and onions. Elevating the taste of the dish by bringing in a rich slightly creamy profile, Eggs are then poached within the sauce. In a classic Middle Eastern fashion, the dish is topped off with a nice spicing from cumin, paprika, and cayenne pepper.
